# Brand Guidelines

## Palette
Deep indigo, warm amber, slate, terracotta.

## Light
Warm raking golden-hour light with long soft shadows.

## Composition
Off-center hero subject, generous negative space, shallow depth of field.

## Subject
One realistic photographic subject, authentic handcrafted materials.

## Never
No neon, fluorescent colors (cyan, magenta), watermarks, logos, ring light, or 3D renders.
